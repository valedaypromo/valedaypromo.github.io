Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026 · Versão em português

Vale Day Promo is a Brazilian deals curator. We share product finds from stores such as Shopee, Amazon and Mercado Livre on our website valedaypromo.github.io, in free WhatsApp and Telegram groups, and on our own social media accounts (Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest and TikTok).

1. Our app: "Vale Day Promo Publicador"

The app is an internal tool used only by the Vale Day Promo team. It connects to our own Pinterest account (@valedaypromo) through Pinterest's official OAuth and is used to:

  • create and schedule Pins on our own boards, using images we produce ourselves (product collages and carousel slides);
  • list our own boards to choose where each Pin goes;
  • read basic analytics of our own Pins.

The app does not access other users' accounts, does not read other people's Pins or boards, does not follow, like, comment or message anyone, and is not offered to the public.

2. Data we collect

Our website has no sign-up and does not ask for name, e-mail, phone number or any personal data. We only use the Pinterest Tag to count visits and clicks in aggregate.

The Pinterest access token is stored only on our own computer and server, is never shared with third parties and can be revoked at any time in Pinterest settings.

3. Affiliate links

Product links are affiliate links: when someone buys through them, the store pays us a small commission at no extra cost to the buyer. Clicking a link takes you to the store's website, which has its own privacy policy.

4. Your rights (Brazilian LGPD)

You can leave our groups at any time. For any data request, contact us on Instagram @valedaypromo.

5. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
